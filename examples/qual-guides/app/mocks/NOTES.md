# Interview companion: phone mocks (brief 02)

## Purpose
Pictures of a small phone app that sits beside the person running an interview, so the owner can decide whether to build it. Plain HTML, about 390 px wide, no scripts, no outside requests. Buttons and checkboxes do nothing; only the Back/Next links move between screens. Open `index.html`.

## Source of the wording
The car scent guide is not written yet, so all wording comes from the Larkspur sample guide (`projects/larkspur-tool-library/guide.md`), and the layout from `projects/_template/guide-layout.md`. Every screen carries a yellow "EXAMPLE CONTENT" banner. The Larkspur guide's volunteer name ("Mara") is a sample name; no real names, numbers or addresses appear.

## The screens
1. **Before you start**: tick-list from the guide's part 7 "Before" card lines, plus "consent words to hand".
2. **Consent**: part 3 script in big type, Yes / No buttons. The two bracketed lines the committee has not confirmed are left out and flagged, as the guide says.
3. **Question**: Q1 with its follow-ups (the guide's "Probe" lines, called follow-ups as the card does), minutes left for 5.1, and the next part. The 5:40 is made up; the part's 8 minutes come from the guide.
4. **Quick answers**: tap buttons for group, how they got the tool, how long ago. These facts are invented by me (marked on screen); the sample guide has no such fixed-answer questions. The brief's scent example (last scent bought, where, how long ago) would fit the car scent guide once it exists.
5. **Wrap-up**: C1 and the part 6 closing steps as a tick-list, plus "straight after" from the card.
6. **Tally**: counts per answer with the quotes behind each, tap to open. All counts and quotes are invented.

## Kept from the guide layout
Part order (consent, core, closing, card), question numbers (Q1, C1), "Serves goal", the part numbers and minutes, the follow-up lines under a question, the [ask them:] hold on consent, and the card's wording.

## Open questions for the owner
1. Should the app show only the questions the person's group is asked (skipping the other group's), or the whole guide?
2. Should the app time the parts itself (a running countdown) or only show the planned minutes?
3. Is a recording button inside the app wanted, or should the interviewer use a separate recorder?
4. Who types the quick-answer buttons: written into the guide by the guide writer, or set up by the interviewer?
5. Should the tally count be tapped in live, or built from the interviewer's notes afterwards?
6. Do conversation notes stay on the phone, or go to a shared place (and then who may see them)?
