Step 2 of 6 - part 3 of the guide, 3 minConsent
EXAMPLE CONTENT - sample wording from the Larkspur sample guide, not a real interview

Read aloud, word for word. Wait for a yes before recording.

"Thank you for making time. My name is Mara and I help at the Larkspur Tool Library. We are talking to neighbours to learn how people get tools for jobs at home, so we can decide how to improve things. Taking part is your choice. You can skip any question, and you can stop at any time, and you will still get the voucher. There are no right or wrong answers; we are only interested in what you really do and think. I would like to record our chat so I can listen again and not miss anything. Is it all right if I record?"
Held back: two lines about who hears the recording and deleting it stay hidden until the committee confirms them (the guide's [ask them:] marker).

Their answer

Yes - recordingNo - notes only

After a yes, say: "It is recording now." After a no, carry on with written notes only, and say so.
