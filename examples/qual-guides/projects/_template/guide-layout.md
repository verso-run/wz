# The layout every guide follows

The guide writer copies this layout; `python3 tools/qg.py guide-check` checks it. Replace everything in angle brackets.

```markdown
# Discussion guide: <project name>

Total time: <N> minutes
For: <who runs the interview, in a few words>

## 1. What we want to learn
1. <a goal in plain words: something you do not know yet>
2. <...>

## 2. Who to talk to
<how many people, who, who not, how to invite them, any thank-you>

## 3. Consent and recording script (read aloud) — <N> min
> "<the words to read out loud, and a pause for a yes before recording starts>"
<one line: this script is for ordinary small-organisation conversations; for a university, school or health
service ask their ethics contact>

## 4. Warm-up — <N> min
W1. <an easy, open question about the person's own life, not about your project>

## 5. Core questions — <N> min total
### 5.1 <topic> — <N> min
Serves goal: <number>
Q1. <one open question about what they actually did or do>
- Probe: <a neutral follow-up>
- Probe: <another>

### 5.2 <topic> — <N> min
Q2. <...>
- Probe: <...>

## 6. Closing — <N> min
C1. <a last open question, such as whether there is anything that was not covered>
<thank them, say what happens next, how to reach you, any thank-you>

## 7. How to run this interview (card)
<at most 30 lines: before, during, things not to do, what to do if, straight after>

## 8. Notes from the writer
<what you changed from the draft questions in the brief and why; anything marked [ask them: ...]; why any flagged question was kept>
```

Rules for the layout: every part has its minutes in the heading (— 5 min), and the Total time line equals the sum of parts 3 to 6.
Questions are numbered Q1, Q2... (core), W1... (warm-up), C1... (closing). Every core question has at least one
`- Probe:` line. At most 10 core questions.
