# Audience — (project name)

*Who you want to talk to. The coordinator asks; you can also fill it in yourself.*

## 1. Who are they? (age range is fine, jobs, where they live, how they came across you)

## 2. Who should NOT be asked? (people too close to the project, for example)

## 3. How will you reach them and invite them?

## 4. Where and how will you talk? (in person, phone, video; the place; anything people need to be able to take part)

## 5. What words do these people use for what you do? And which words would puzzle them?

## 6. Will you offer a thank-you?
