# Membership summary (made up for this example)

- 180 paid-up members at the end of last year; 61 joined in the first year we opened, 22 joined last year.
- Loans in a typical month: about 55, made by about 40 different members.
- 94 members have not borrowed anything in the last twelve months.
- The five most borrowed items: cordless drill, step ladder, wallpaper stripper, hedge trimmer, pressure washer.
- Opening hours: Saturday 10:00 to 13:00 and Wednesday 18:00 to 20:00, run by two volunteers each time.
- Annual fee: £20 (£10 for people on a low income, no questions asked). Late return fee: none.
