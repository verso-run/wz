# Project brief — Larkspur Tool Library

(Larkspur Tool Library is a made-up organisation, used as the worked example for qual-guides. Nothing here is real.)

## 1. What is this project, in two sentences?

The Larkspur Tool Library is a volunteer-run lending shelf in the back room of Larkspur Community Hall: neighbours pay
£20 a year and can borrow drills, ladders, garden tools and similar. We have 180 members but only about 40 borrow in a
typical month, and we want to understand why, and why more of the neighbours who know about us have not joined.

## 2. What will you decide or do differently depending on what you learn?

The volunteer committee meets in January to decide how to spend a small grant (£1,800): extra opening sessions, a small
delivery service, a bigger range of tools, or something else. We also want to know what to say on our next leaflet.

## 3. What do you want to find out?

1. How do neighbours currently get hold of a tool they need once or twice (borrow, buy, hire, ask a friend, do without)?
2. What did people who joined and then stopped borrowing run into?
3. What has stopped neighbours who have heard of us from walking in?

## 4. What do you already believe or hope is true?

Mostly we think the opening hours are the problem (Saturday 10 to 1 and Wednesday 6 to 8 in the evening). Some of us
also think people are put off by the £20 fee, and that people do not know what we have in stock. We would love it if
the answer were "just add a weekday session".

## 5. Anything that must not be asked about, or is sensitive?

Please do not ask about money or income directly: it is a mixed neighbourhood. Do not ask about people's housing
situation. People may mention health or mobility; follow their lead and do not press.

## 6. How many conversations can you have, and how long can each one be?

Six to eight, in person or on the phone, 40 minutes at most each. We have six weeks.

## 7. Who will run the conversations, and how much have they done this before?

Mara Quill (a volunteer, and the person who runs the Wednesday session). She has never run an interview. She is warm
and a good listener, but tends to jump in and help when someone is stuck.
