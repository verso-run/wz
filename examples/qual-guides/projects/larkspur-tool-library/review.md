## Goals and questions
- Goal 1, how neighbours get a tool they need once or twice: Q1 and Q2 with their follow-ups. Covered.
- Goal 2, what people who joined and stopped borrowing ran into: Q5 and Q6 (Group B), with Q7 and Q8 for everyone. Covered. The new Q6 follow-ups let the person say their own reasons.
- Goal 3, what has stopped neighbours who have heard of the shelf from coming in: Q3 and Q4 (Group A), with Q7, Q8 and Q9. Covered.
- Order: warm-up, facts (Q1 to Q8), the one opinion question (Q9), then the closing. Follows M2 ("start with the factual questions") and M1.
- Probes: all neutral ("tell me more", "what happened", "can you give me an example"); none puts words in the person's mouth (M1, M2).
- Timing: 3 + 3 + 8 + 6 (or 6) + 10 + 4 = 34 minutes, re-added by me; each person gets only one of 5.2 or 5.3. Six minutes inside the 40-minute limit (M1) and the closing question is kept (M2).
