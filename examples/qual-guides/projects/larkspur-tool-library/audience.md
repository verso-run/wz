# Audience — Larkspur Tool Library

## 1. Who are they?

Adults who live within about a mile of Larkspur Community Hall. We want two groups: (a) people who have **never**
joined, though most have seen the leaflet or the noticeboard, and (b) people who **joined and have not borrowed for a
year**. Mixed ages, many renters, a good number of retired neighbours, some new parents.

## 2. Who should NOT be asked?

Current volunteers and committee members. People who borrowed in the last three months (they are our regulars and we
already hear from them).

## 3. How will you reach them and invite them?

A short note through letterboxes on the three nearest streets, a card on the hall noticeboard, and a personal message
from Mara to the lapsed members whose contact details we hold. We offer a £10 voucher for the hardware shop on
Alder Road as a thank-you.

## 4. Where and how will you talk?

At the hall's small meeting room, at the person's home if they prefer, or by phone. The hall has a step at the front
door, so we will offer the phone or a home visit to anyone who finds steps hard.

## 5. What words do these people use? Which words would puzzle them?

They say "borrow", "the tool shelf", "the hall". They do not say "membership model", "inventory", "onboarding" or
"stakeholders". Many would say "DIY" and "doing jobs around the house".

## 6. Will you offer a thank-you?

Yes, the £10 hardware-shop voucher, given whether or not the person answers every question.
