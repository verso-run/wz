## Goals and questions
- Goal 1 (how they ended up with the scent): Q1, Q2, Q5 probe "Where did you get it from?". Covered.
- Goal 2 (last time a scent wore off, or they stopped using one): Q4, Q5, Q6 and Q7. Covered.
- Goal 3 (what they do about smells at other times; buy or put up with): Q8 (probe "Tell me about a time you left a smell as it was") and Q9. Covered by neutral questions.
- Goal 4 (words for the smell): Q3, "What does the smell of the one in your car now remind you of, if anything?" It asks what it reminds them of and offers no word list (M11 supports the caution). Covered, and no longer optional.
- Decision in the brief (refill subscription, seasonal range, cheaper starter pack): no question asks anyone to predict or choose between the three (M7, M8). The interviews speak mostly to refills; the writer says so.
- Order: warm-up, what they got, what happened when it stopped, other smells, closing. Facts first, opinions after (M2).
- Past behaviour (M7, M8, M10): every core question asks for the last time; the card forbids "would you pay", "would you buy" and "do you like". The window for recall is the most recent time (M10).
- Number and length of conversations (M9, M12): up to eight, about four per group, 30 minutes at most, three weeks. This fits the brief's six to eight. The card says to read notes after the 6th, add a 7th and 8th only if stories still differ, and ask the owner before going past eight. Notes on who was asked and who agreed are kept (M12).
- Timing: 2 + 2 + 18 + 3 = 25 minutes for people asked to give 30; the sections inside add up (5 + 8 + 5). It is tight for a first-time interviewer: nine questions in 18 minutes, with Q4 to Q7 getting 8 minutes. The card's rule (drop the last probe on each question) makes it believable, and the writer names the tightness (M1, M2).
- Consent script: why you are asking, recording only after a clear yes, handwritten notes if no, skipping and stopping, no wrong answers, who hears it, how long things are kept, names not used, deletion on request, voucher regardless. All match docs/shop-notes.md (M2).
- Probes: all short and neutral.
