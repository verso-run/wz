# Audience — Tumbledown Car Scents

## 1. Who are they?

Adults who drive regularly and have **bought any car air freshener in the last twelve months**, ours or anyone's. Mixed
ages (mostly 25 to 60), mostly the main driver of the car. We want two groups: (a) people who bought from us once and did
not buy again, and (b) people who bought something else, from a petrol station or a supermarket, and never tried us.

## 2. Who should NOT be asked?

Family, friends and anyone who works in car sales, valeting or the fragrance trade. People who bought from us more than
twice (they already write to us).

## 3. How will you reach them and invite them?

A short, plain email from Rowan to the one-time customers whose address we hold, and a post in a local drivers' group
for group (b). We offer a £10 voucher for the shop as a thank-you.

## 4. Where and how will you talk?

By phone or video, at a time that suits the person, nothing to install. Recording only with a yes.

## 5. What words do these people use? Which words would puzzle them?

They say "air freshener", "car smell", "the thing hanging off my mirror", "vanilla", "pine", "fresh". They do not say
"top notes", "olfactive", "SKU", "subscription box", "refill cycle" or "scent profile".

## 6. Will you offer a thank-you?

Yes, the £10 voucher, given whether or not the person answers every question.
