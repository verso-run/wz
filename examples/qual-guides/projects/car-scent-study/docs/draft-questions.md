# Draft questions from the owners (made up for this example)

Questions Rowan and the other co-owner wrote down before the brief, as they wrote them:

1. "Would you pay £4 a month for a refill subscription so you never run out?"
2. "Don't you think seasonal scents are a great idea?"
3. "How do you feel about our scents and our packaging?"
4. "Why did you not buy a second one, since it only costs £6?"
5. "Would you buy more if we had more scents?"
6. "Do you leave the windows open as well as using an air freshener?"

Rowan said the worry is "I will not know what to say if they go quiet". The other co-owner said we should "just ask them
what they want".
