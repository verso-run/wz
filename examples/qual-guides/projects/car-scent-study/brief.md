# Project brief — Tumbledown Car Scents

(Tumbledown Scent Co. is a made-up business, used as the worked example for the research-and-mocks chain. Nothing here
is real, and none of it comes from anyone's actual study.)

## 1. What is this project, in two sentences?

Tumbledown Scent Co. is a two-person online shop that makes vent-clip car air fresheners: a small wooden disc soaked in
fragrance oil, £6 each, in four scents. About a fifth of our customers buy a second one; most do not come back, and we
do not know why.

## 2. What will you decide or do differently depending on what you learn?

In March we spend £2,500 on one of three things: a refill subscription, a bigger seasonal range, or a cheaper
single-scent starter pack. We also want to know what to put on the product page.

## 3. What do you want to find out?

1. How people end up with the scent that is in their car now (who chose it, where they bought it, what else they tried).
2. What happened the last time a scent wore off, or the last time someone stopped using one.
3. What people do about smells in the car at other times (windows, sprays, cleaning), and whether they think of it as
   something to buy or something to put up with.

## 4. What do you already believe or hope is true?

We think people want a "set and forget" refill subscription, and that seasonal scents are popular. We would love it if
people said they would happily pay £4 a month.

## 5. Anything that must not be asked about, or is sensitive?

Please do not ask about smoking, pets or children's travel sickness unless the person raises them. Do not ask about
income. Allergies and headaches may come up: follow their lead and do not press.

## 6. How many conversations can you have, and how long can each one be?

Six to eight, by phone or video, 30 minutes at most each. We have three weeks.

## 7. Who will run the conversations, and how much have they done this before?

Rowan Pell (a made-up co-owner, who packs the orders and answers customer emails). Rowan has never run an interview.
Rowan is friendly and quick to explain the product when someone hesitates, which tends to put words in their mouth.
